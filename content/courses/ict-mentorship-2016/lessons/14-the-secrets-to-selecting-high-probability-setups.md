- No charts — a "micro trading plan". The real secret is PROCESS-ORIENTED, BINARY thinking (do X or Y), not hunting entries; reactive/impulsive thinking breeds emotional trading. Order: patience → define trade environments → trade parameters → executable criteria (what negates the idea?) → understand WHY the trade should work (logged demo experience).
- Ignore outside opinions (forums, Twitter, friends); your model is yours.
- THREE PERSPECTIVES must agree for a high-reward setup:
- 1. BIG PICTURE (need ≥2 of 4): macro analysis (inflationary vs deflationary), interest-rate analysis (trend/hikes/cuts/surprises, rate differentials and carry), intermarket analysis (CRB index vs Dollar Index — usually inverse), seasonal influences.
- 2. INTERMEDIATE (need ≥2 of 3): top-down analysis (monthly/weekly/daily key levels, highs/lows, OBs — funds analyse monthly/weekly and execute on the daily), COT data (CFTC weekly report; commercials at 12-month or 4-year extremes hint at a turn; ignore small speculators), market sentiment (extremes; e.g. Market Vane, barchart.com opinion — the least significant).
- 3. SHORT-TERM (need ONE from EACH of 3): correlation analysis (DXY SMT "crack in correlation"; correlated-pair SMT, e.g. EUR/USD vs GBP/USD), time &amp; price theory (quarterly effect, monthly effect, WEEKLY RANGE — ICT is a weekly-range "one shot one kill" trader — daily range / Power of 3, time of day), IPDA (institutional order flow, liquidity, the market efficiency paradigm).
- That's SEVEN things in agreement (2 + 2 + 3) for a high-reward setup — still not an entry signal.
- Big-picture and intermediate homework is done at the weekend (ICT's Saturday studies); the short-term perspective changes daily. Most losses come from the short-term perspective (forcing trades, or the market in transition).
- Smart money doesn't only return to "its" orders (as supply/demand teaching claims); it seeks existing orders (stops) to engineer counterparties.
- The end goal of the mentorship: a flowchart for each trading model. Trading should be boring and routine — no excitement during the week. ICT cites his ~59% October 2016 result (myfxbook).
