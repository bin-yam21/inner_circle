- Two parts of a setup: (1) the context/framework — the market's condition — and (2) the ICT tool you pair with it.
- Only four market conditions exist, and each pairs with one tool:
- 1. EXPANSION (price leaves equilibrium quickly; market makers reveal their repricing) → ORDER BLOCK. Don't chase; wait for the return to the OB left near/at equilibrium. Example: consolidation → expansion → buy at the last down candle → 100+ pips.
- 2. RETRACEMENT (price moves back inside the recent range to reprice inefficient levels) → FAIR VALUE GAPS / LIQUIDITY VOIDS ("Trade Inside the Range", 2012 webinar).
- 3. REVERSAL (stops have been run; a significant move should unfold the other way) → LIQUIDITY POOLS above old highs / below old lows. USD/CHF is choppy and good for turtle soups/false breaks.
- 4. CONSOLIDATION (clear range, no willingness to move; orders build on both sides) → EQUILIBRIUM (the midpoint, defined using candle BODIES). Expect expansion, then a return to equilibrium.
- Cycle: consolidation → expansion → retracement or consolidation → (reversal) → ... Every move starts from consolidation; the side with more money gets absorbed. Wait for the first expansion to get the clue.
- The Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm (IPDA): ICT says ~90% of FX pricing is done by electronic algorithms, so the market is manipulated and its "fingerprints" are visible.
- Prerequisites: ICT's free Market Maker Series, Precision Trading Concepts and Sniper Series.
- You only need ONE condition + tool to become consistent; four major pairs with one setup gives roughly a trade a day. Homework: label past examples of each condition.
