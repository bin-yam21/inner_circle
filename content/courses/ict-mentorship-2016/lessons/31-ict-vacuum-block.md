- Bullish vacuum block = a gap in price created by a volatility event (NFP, FOMC/rates news, unscheduled geopolitical shock, or a futures/stock session open). No trades occur between the previous close and the new open — a "vacuum of liquidity". NFP can gap 30–60 pips.
- Treat the gap like a candle: it has a high, a low and a mean threshold.
- Context: gap UP from a discount when you expect higher prices. After a long rally, a gap may instead be an exhaustion gap (capitulation).
- Two outcomes when price comes back down: (a) it stops at a bullish OB (the higher of the down candles before the gap) and leaves part of the gap open → that remainder becomes a fair value gap / breakaway gap; (b) it fills the gap 100% to the close of the last up candle before the gap = "perfect delivery of price", then can drive higher.
- Time of day decides: an 8:30 a.m. NY gap with lots of session left is likely to be filled during the day; a gap after 10–11 a.m. NY often stays open that day and is filled later.
- Rule after the fill: once the gap is closed and price rallies through the gap-candle, price should NOT go back below that first up candle's close. If it does, take profit / exit — the trade is suspect.
- Bearish vacuum block = mirror (gap down, fill with up candles, then short).
