- Mirror of the discount lesson. Premium = relative to the CURRENT range, not an indicator ("we don't need overbought/oversold indicators").
- Find an impulse price swing (several small swings can form one larger impulse leg). Draw the Fib from the swing high to the swing low.
- Equilibrium = 50%. ICT does NOT sell at 50% (unlike other teachers). Price must move above 50% into premium; the sell zone is the OTE 62–79% (70.5% sweet spot).
- If price never gets above 50%, there's no trade on that swing — let it go.
- Swing points need only 3 candles (he does not use the 5-bar Williams fractal); watch for the 4th candle to show direction.
- Turtle soup sell inside premium: price above equilibrium AND running stops above an old high = high probability. Take profits below established swing lows; keep part for the next low.
- Stop-outs will happen; if stopped, look for the next run above an old high while still in premium.
- Consolidations: trade turtle soups using premium/discount. Bearish order block (last up candle) inside the OTE adds confluence.
- Analogy: you sell a car at a premium, not a discount. Sell above old highs where buy stops = willing buyers.
- Daily examples: short near the 98 big figure, cover 10 pips below the 97.39 low (~70 pips); another &gt;100 pips. Hourly: short 97.70, covers at 42 and 60 pips (+10 below the low = 70).
- You don't need a daily bias: ranges always exist (trending, consolidating or reversing), and you can trade inside them.
