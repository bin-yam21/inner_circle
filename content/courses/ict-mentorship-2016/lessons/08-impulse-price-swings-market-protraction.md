- Impulse price swings: read price as a chain of swings (high → low → high...). Each swing can be measured for premium/discount.
- Market protraction = an impulse price swing that is TIME-SENSITIVE: a small move counter to the day's real direction, designed to manipulate (a "Judas swing").
- Three protraction moments every 24 hours: (1) around 0:00 GMT / Asia (ICT: Asia not very influential), (2) right after midnight New York (London) — e.g. an initial rally after the midnight candle on a bearish day, then the real drop, (3) after 7:00 a.m. New York — a retracement against the London move before New York continues it.
- Rule: if the protraction move goes up, think the opposite (down), and vice versa; it draws traders onto the wrong side or reaches for liquidity.
- Worked example: impulse swing down → retrace into premium at the 62% level → sell, aiming for stops below the swing low and older equal lows; the expansion ran to the 32.55 level because an old low sat there.
- Use for session drills: impulse swings + time of day builds anticipation skills.
