- Definition: analysing correlated / inversely correlated assets to see what smart money is accumulating or distributing. In FX: compare every price swing in the US Dollar Index (DXY) with the pair you trade.
- SMT = "smart money tool/technique" divergence.
- SYMMETRICAL conditions (DXY lower low ↔ currency higher high, or DXY higher high ↔ currency lower low) CONFIRM the trend → don't stalk reversals; a pop above a short-term high is just a run on stops before continuation (lets you anticipate turtle soups).
- NON-SYMMETRICAL = USDX SMT: e.g. DXY makes a lower low but the currency FAILS to make a higher high (or the currency makes a higher high while DXY fails to make a lower low) → trend unlikely to continue; stalking reversals is high probability.
- GBP/USD daily, late April → mid-June 2016: cable made a higher high (ran buy stops above ~1.4760, nearly to 1.5065) while DXY did NOT make a lower low (held ~93.80–94.00) → bullish DXY SMT → cable "tanked". ICT was a dollar bull for all of H2 2016 because of this.
- Late Aug → first week of Sept 2016: cable higher high above old highs, DXY no lower low → cable distributed at old highs ("retail candy land" for breakout buyers), dollar accumulated.
- Logic: if the dollar won't go lower it's being bought aggressively; otherwise they would wait for lower prices.
- Tool: an MT4 "overlay chart line" indicator to overlay DXY on the pair (from the mentorship forum).
- Once you have this daily bias: short-term traders sell every 60-minute/4-hour bearish OB; day traders sell above the New York midnight opening price.
