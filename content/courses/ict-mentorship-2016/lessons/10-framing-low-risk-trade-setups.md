- Select setups on higher timeframes (daily/weekly/monthly give bias, institutional order flow and key levels; banks analyse there). Higher-timeframe setups form slowly — suitable for people with jobs.
- Lower the risk by transposing the higher-timeframe level to lower timeframes → smaller stop, same idea.
- AUD/USD example: daily bullish OB at 0.7512 (banks bought there before). Price ran below an old low into 0.7512 (turtle soup long — sell stops below an old low pair with smart-money buys).
  - Hourly framing (previous lesson): long at 0.7542, stop under the OB's mean threshold ≈ 20 pips.
  - 15-minute: bullish OB off the 0.7512 level → buy 0.7520, stop 0.7507 = 17 pips, much nearer the key level.
  - 5-minute: a down candle hitting 0.7512 gets violated → buy ~0.7515 (0.7512 + spread), stop 0.7507 = 8 pips. Price reached 3R (+24 pips) before even taking the 5-minute buy stops, and was already at the hourly entry 0.7542.
- Warning: ultra-tight stops only work if you understand WHY price should react at the level.
