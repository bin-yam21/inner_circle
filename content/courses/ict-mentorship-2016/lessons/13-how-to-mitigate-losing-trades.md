- Scenario: long at a bullish OB with the stop just under the mean threshold → stopped out for a full 2% loss (more than 2% = "gambler").
- A stop-out doesn't kill the idea. Re-evaluate: price traded above a new down candle → that candle is a new bullish OB → re-enter at its open/top with the stop BELOW the whole order block (more room).
- KEY RULE: the re-entry uses HALF the previous risk (2% loss → next trade 1%; 1% loss → 0.5%).
- With half risk you need only 2R to fully recover the loss; at 3R you are at a new equity high.
- Developing traders: when the loss is recovered (2R), take the trade off — especially late in the week (Thursday/Friday). Don't carry a loss into the weekend. Advanced: lock the stop at break-even-to-equity so the drawdown can't return, then let it run; ~3R is a sensible final exit.
- Multiple losses: keep cutting size (2% → 1% → 0.5%); one 3R winner at reduced size still repays most of it. One trade doesn't have to erase all losses.
- "Equity preservation is the number one rule." Increasing size after a loss = throwing good money after bad and leads to fear-based trading; any loss could be the start of a 10-trade losing streak.
