- Buy stop rules: monthly/weekly order flow points to a PD array ABOVE daily price (e.g. weekly bearish OB or weekly/monthly FVG above). Daily must CLOSE a down candle. Place a buy stop at that down candle's OPEN. "Using strength to get you long."
- Ties to order block theory: a down candle in an uptrend is a bullish OB; trading back through its open confirms. No fill → move the order to the next down candle. Keep doing this until the monthly/weekly premium PD array is reached.
- Prefer entries at or below the equilibrium of the monthly/weekly range; the higher into premium, the weaker these signals get.
- Re-entry: after several hundred pips, take partial profits; if price returns to the same open, put the partial back on at the same price.
- Sell stop: mirror — monthly/weekly pointing lower, daily closes an UP candle, sell stop at its open.
- Example: USD/JPY pierced the 2007 old high near 123.50, rejected, broke market structure; five up-candle opens each triggered shorts as it fell; several hundred pips each and one with over 1,000 pips of downside. Those up candles later acted as bearish OBs for adding.
- Stops go below the most recent daily swing low (details in Lesson 8 / trade management).
