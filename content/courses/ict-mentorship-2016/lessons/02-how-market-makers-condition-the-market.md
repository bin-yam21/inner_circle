- The market efficiency paradigm drawing: a big circle of uninformed/retail money (social-media gurus, indicator traders — ICT was one, teaching stochastics/RSI/Williams %R in AOL chat rooms) believes its size drives price through supply and demand, trend lines, moving-average crosses — "a facade". A small, quiet circle of smart money (banks) is the actual "drive shaft". "Are you a lamb or a lion?"
- Price delivery is an algorithm (AI) that knows human behaviour (fear and greed); brokers also widen spreads.
- DAILY TEMPLATE using the four conditions (bullish day example, NY time):
- 1. Asian range = CONSOLIDATION/equilibrium (orders build).
- 2. After midnight = MANIPULATION / Judas swing (false move down) — EXPANSION, then the London REVERSAL (stop run; the London low / "higher low" of the day).
- 3. Range EXPANSION into ~5:00 a.m.
- 4. 5:00–8:00 consolidation; 8:00–8:30 RETRACEMENT (news embargo lift = liquidity injection).
- 5. New York: REVERSAL or another EXPANSION into 10–11 a.m.
- 6. London close (10–11 a.m.) = REVERSAL, then CONSOLIDATION for the rest of the day.
- RULES of sequence: consolidation is ALWAYS followed by EXPANSION (never straight to retracement or reversal). After expansion → either RETRACEMENT (back into the order block, then another leg the same way) or REVERSAL.
- The same pattern shows on the weekly range: Sunday open consolidation → Monday expansion → reversal Monday/Tuesday → expansion → midweek consolidation → reversal or retracement.
- ICT gave ~50–60 pip examples daily during the mentorship, while stressing patience and suppressing the urge to make money.
- Intermarket hint: watch EUR/GBP to see which of EUR/USD or GBP/USD is being held back.
