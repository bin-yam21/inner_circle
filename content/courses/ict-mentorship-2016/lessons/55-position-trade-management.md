- Checklist (bullish; mirror for bearish):
- 1. A seasonal tendency expected for the next 3–4 months (a roadmap only).
- 2. Intermarket confirmation — stocks, rates, commodities, currencies; at least 3 of 4 agree.
- 3. Yields moving in favour of the currency; yield divergence warns of a pause.
- 4. Monthly/weekly PD arrays define the objective.
- 5. Expect a quarterly shift / intermediate swing; aim for the middle "meat", not the exact top/bottom.
- 6. Daily PD arrays frame the entry (OB, void, gap, rejection block, old high/low).
- 7. Choose a stop entry (filled more often, wider risk) or a limit entry (better price, may miss).
- Risk no more than 1%: "big positions in time, not in size".
- TRAILING STOP using IPDA data ranges: below the LOWEST LOW OF THE LAST 40 TRADING DAYS (shorts: above the 40-day high) — avoids premature stop-outs; don't rush to break-even in long-term trading ("the worst thing"). Once the move has covered roughly half to three-quarters of the expected weekly/monthly range, tighten to the 20-day low/high. (ICT's example switches at the 50% level; his wording also says ¾.)
- USD/JPY short example (weekly bearish OB, open 121.69 / high 121.72): sell, stop above the 40-day high (~260 pips), reached about 8R (8 × 260 pips) toward the weekly bullish OB.
- USD/JPY long example: buys around the weekly bullish OB before/after the US election; the 40-day low (109.19) held through the election whipsaw; above the range's 50% the stop trailed under the 20-day low until the weekly bearish OB objective was hit.
