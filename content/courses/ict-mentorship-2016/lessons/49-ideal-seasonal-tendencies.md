- "Ideal" = the currency's futures seasonal and the Dollar Index seasonal are most diametrically opposed at the same time, AND the market is already predisposed that way. A tendency, not a guarantee.
- AUD/USD: AUD futures rally March → top in May while DXY declines March → May. Best in a long-term bull market or after a long-term low.
- NZD/USD: low around March–April, rally into May; DXY high in April, low in May.
- EUR/USD: seasonal LOW in June–July (DXY high then).
- GBP/USD: low in March, high in May (DXY high March–April, low May); 40-year and 15-year data agree.
- USD/CHF: DXY seasonal HIGH June–July; Swiss franc LOW June–July.
- USD/JPY: DXY high around March–April then sells off into May; yen low March–April → sell USD/JPY if the dollar is bearish or the yen bullish.
- USD/CAD: DXY high March–April, low May; CAD low March–April, high May → sell USD/CAD when bearish dollar or bullish CAD (only ONE needs to be trending).
- Habit: start every trading day with the macro view; keep a calendar of "things to watch this month / next month". Seasonals are reused in swing and short-term trading.
