- Avoid: rushing for huge pip or percent gains ("Forex is not the lottery"); large risk hoping for large returns; assuming small risk can't grow an account (compounding does the work — even $100 is fine to start); sacrificing equity through poor planning. Risk ≤ 2% (ideally less for new traders).
- "Nobody goes broke taking profits; they go broke taking too much risk." Greed puts you in a trade, then it turns into fear — respect the risk side first.
- Win rate vs minimum reward:risk to break even: 75% → very low R needed; 50% → 1:1; 40% → 1.5:1; 33% → 2:1; 25% → 3:1. With 3:1 setups you can be wrong 75% of the time and still profit; as accuracy grows, profits grow exponentially.
- ICT's sample month (book shown in the mentorship): $5,000 → +50% (+$2,500) from only 10 trades, average 51.8 pips, 518 pips total. His usual target is 50–75 pips a week; trying for more gives him a "King Kong" feeling (and falls).
- STARTER GOAL: 6% a month compounded (doubles the account in a year; $1,000 → ~$2,000; kept up for 10 years without adding money → over $1,000,000). Needs ONE trade a week of 20 pips with a 20-pip stop at 1.5% risk (1:1). Don't force a trade every day.
- Example (AUD/USD daily OB at 0.7512 → hourly): daily bullish OB (open-to-high = FVG); an hourly run below an old low into 0.7512 (turtle soup); wait for confirmation (bank sponsorship) — price traded through the down candle → buy limit at its open + 5-pip spread ≈ 0.7542, stop 0.7522 (20 pips, below the mean threshold). 1R = +1.5% (could close for the week). Better: scale out at each 20-pip multiple; stop to break-even after 2R; targets = buy stops above equal highs; it reached 5R, "a one shot one kill". Half off at 1R + half held to 5R ≈ 4%+.
