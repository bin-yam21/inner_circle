- ICT's premise: markets aren't random — they are "100% engineered" by an Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm (IPDA) that works within data ranges; that's how levels can be called "to the pip".
- QUARTERLY SHIFT: every 3–4 months the market must generate new interest — the prevailing swing reverses, retraces deeply, or consolidates (in strong trends, a consolidation or a quick run below a daily short-term low). Always analyse monthly, weekly and daily and anticipate intermediate swings; take partial profits before them and re-add afterwards.
- BUY PROGRAM (accumulation) vs SELL PROGRAM (distribution) — compare the UNDERLYING (the pair) with the BENCHMARK (Dollar Index), daily chart only:
  - Accumulation examples: DXY makes a lower low while USD/CHF (or USD/JPY) makes a HIGHER low; EUR/USD (or GBP/USD) makes a lower low while DXY makes a LOWER HIGH (→ turtle soup long); DXY makes a higher high while EUR/USD makes a HIGHER LOW (→ DXY turtle soup sell).
  - Distribution: DXY higher high while USD/CHF/USD/JPY makes a LOWER high; GBP/EUR makes a higher high while DXY makes a HIGHER LOW (→ turtle soup sell); DXY lower low while GBP/EUR makes a LOWER HIGH.
  - USD/CHF, USD/JPY and USD/CAD (when oil isn't the driver) correlate WITH DXY; EUR/USD and GBP/USD are inverse.
- LOOK BACK: put a vertical line on the FIRST TRADING DAY OF THE MOST RECENT COMPLETED MONTH (e.g. on 14 Nov 2016 use 1 Oct 2016). Look back 20, 40 and 60 TRADING days (the algorithm reaches ~3 months back). Identify the order flow (trading higher or lower over those 60 days), the significant high/low, old highs/lows (liquidity), rejection blocks (bodies vs wicks), OBs, FVGs and voids. Re-anchor the line to the obvious intermediate high/low that marks the last market structure shift.
- CAST FORWARD: project 20, 40 and 60 trading days to the right — the next setup/shift is expected within that window (up to 60 days of waiting). On MT4, drag a trend line from the vertical line to count bars.
- Example: DXY rallied Oct → early Dec 2015 (order flow bullish; liquidity below the October low). After 1 Dec 2015 a key low broke → bearish quarterly expectation into March 2016; DXY's new highs only filled a void while EUR/USD made HIGHER LOWS (accumulation); the EUR/USD high landed right at the 60-day cast-forward. Mid-2016: DXY lower lows while EUR/USD failed to make higher highs (distribution). US election day sat ~60 days out and gave a EUR/USD spike high then a sharp sell-off (DXY didn't make a lower low → dollar accumulation).
- Homework: repeat the exercise from any starting month (e.g. July 2016) on your own charts.
