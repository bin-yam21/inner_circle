- Classic H&amp;S: shoulder–head–shoulder, neckline; target = head-to-neckline height projected from the neckline. Inverted H&amp;S = mirror. These form genuinely only at intermediate/long-term highs/lows, but retail forces them on lower timeframes.
- ICT doesn't pick long-term tops/bottoms. He uses the pattern AGAINST retail when the higher-timeframe institutional order flow points the other way.
- Bullish HTF + H&amp;S on a lower timeframe: the break of the neckline/equal lows = turtle soup long (sell stops below the clean lows) → BUY; first target the buy stops above the head; first profit possible at the right shoulder.
- Bearish HTF + inverted H&amp;S: the break above the neckline = run on buy stops → SELL; target the sell stops below the head.
- GBP/USD daily, Aug 2015: bullish OB (down candle traded through on 10 Aug; high 1.5546); price dipped into its body on 12 Aug. Hourly showed an H&amp;S whose retail target was ~1.5495. ICT: buy ~1.5550 for 1.5620 (+70 pips) after price ran the equal lows several times.
- GBP/USD daily (Oct): price returned into a breaker ~1.5345–1.5350 (bearish). Lower timeframe inverted H&amp;S → sell above the neckline; use the highest candle BODY (1.5502) as the sell level rather than the wick; covered below the "head" low; ~80 pips.
