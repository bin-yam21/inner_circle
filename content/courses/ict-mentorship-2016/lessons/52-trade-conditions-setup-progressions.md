- Applies the PD-array hierarchy to the ~1,200-pip USD/JPY position trade from the Money Management lesson: buy at a monthly/weekly/daily DISCOUNT array and target a PREMIUM array (reverse for shorts).
- Study points: from a premium or discount, markets first look to REBALANCE to the equilibrium of the recent range (just targeting equilibrium gives many trades). At equilibrium, use monthly market structure to choose the side.
- Yen cash price (barchart.com): high in Aug 2016, structure broke bearish; the Trump-election rally ran into a bearish OB and then sold off. USD/JPY is the inverse (USD is the base currency).
- USD/JPY monthly: equal lows (sell stops) + a two-candle bullish OB, equilibrium ~99.00; November 2016 ran below the candle BODIES = monthly REJECTION BLOCK → rally. Above: a monthly breaker (down candle after a run on old-high buy stops) and a gap.
- Weekly: price retested two weekly bullish OBs (higher and lower); targets were an FVG, rejection blocks above equal-bodied candles, and the weekly BEARISH OB (last up candle before the sharp drop): open 118.61, actual high 118.66 — "precision".
- Daily: in an uptrend, focus on the DOWN candles (institutions buy at their creation and again on a return). With three consecutive down candles, the OB starts at the HIGHEST open (113.28; price returned to 113.26). Later re-entries around 113.90/113.00.
- KEY RULE: if a daily bullish PD array fails, drop back to the WEEKLY array; if that fails, the MONTHLY. The election whipsaw broke daily levels but went straight into the lower weekly bullish OB. "If you lose a level on a daily, don't be concerned — go out to a weekly chart." The algorithm works mainly on the daily but reaches into the larger "open float" (weekly ranges) when daily liquidity is used up.
- Keep monthly/weekly levels on every chart in your platform, even as a day trader.
