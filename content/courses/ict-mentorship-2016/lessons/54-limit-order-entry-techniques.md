- Buy limit rules: monthly/weekly suggests bullish institutional order flow toward a PD array ABOVE price; the daily must CLOSE a down candle (not valid while it is forming); place the buy limit at that down candle's close. The next day trading below that close fills you deep in discount.
- Sell limit: monthly/weekly bearish toward PD arrays below; daily closes an up candle; sell limit at its close.
- Not every candle: blend with a daily PD array (order block, gap, filled void, run above a recent high, old support-turned-resistance).
- Example (USD/JPY, 2016): weekly bullish OB near 100.00 (discount) and weekly bearish OB around 118–119 (premium). After the September 2016 retest of an August 2016 OB and the November 2016 post-US-election dip, buying below each down-close daily candle captured moves of about 1,800, 980, 785, 600, 500 and 360 pips into the weekly bearish OB.
- Message: "Do you still think you need intraday trading to make pips?" Long-term trades take time to set up but give large moves with low risk.
