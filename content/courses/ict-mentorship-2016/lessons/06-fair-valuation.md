- Two meanings of fair value: (1) EQUILIBRIUM — the 50% of a defined high–low range; (2) fair value for MARKET MAKERS — where it's fair for them to accumulate (in discount) and to distribute/exit (in premium).
- Buy in the lower third of the current range (discount); sell in the upper third (premium).
- Liquidity void: a fast drop with large ranges and small wicks. A fair value gap inside it = range between up candles where only down movement happened (ICT uses candle bodies). Price later comes back to fill it — it may take time.
- At equilibrium price can go either way: use the most recent market-structure break (here a swing high was broken → bullish).
- AUD/USD (week of 24 Sept 2016), ICT's live call for 0.7665: price ran a short-term high, came back to equilibrium of the small range AND to a bullish OB (down candle before the break), in the lower third / deep discount of the parent swing (even below the 79% retracement) → fair value for market makers to build a net long book. Consolidation around equilibrium (one spike lower didn't break the range) → expansion up → filled the void → reached the FVG (low of the up candle at 0.7675, "to the pip"). He targets ~10 pips before the expected level.
- Retail sees the rally as a buy (at a premium); smart money is distributing there. Scale out of longs bought in discount.
- Market flow: from buy stops to sell stops and back; from fair value → discount → premium → fair value. Ask: are we at a discount, premium or fair value? Are they accumulating, manipulating or distributing?
- "We don't play the breakout game. We anticipate the breakout."
