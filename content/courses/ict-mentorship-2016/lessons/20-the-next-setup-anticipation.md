- Monthly-chart ranges move only with big institutional money; finding where they place orders on the monthly chart means few trades and many pips.
- Homework: on every pair you follow, mark the open, high, low and close of the last THREE monthly candles and carry those levels down to lower timeframes.
- Defining the monthly range: find the most recent DOWN candle, then the UP candle to its left whose low is above that down candle's high. Those two candles bound the range.
- Activation: when price trades above a down candle's high, that down candle becomes a bullish OB; wait for a return to it, then target the last up candle (bearish OB). Mirror for bearish: price breaks an up candle's low → bearish OB; target the most recent down candle.
- USD/CAD example (Nov 2016): monthly down candle high 1.3144; monthly open ~1.3080 respected on the daily; 1.3280 was the downside objective (two weekly down candles = bullish OB) before expansion higher.
- USD/JPY example: range 103.26–106.28 (~300 pips). Below 103.26 = buy zone at the monthly bullish OB. On the US election knee-jerk (Nov 2016) price dipped into the daily bullish OB without challenging its mean threshold → buys → rally toward old/equal highs, 300+ pips.
- NZD/USD example: bearish monthly OB, weekly retest; objective ~0.6983/0.6985 (cover near the 0.7005 body close, ~120 pips), possibly 0.6780 later.
- Process: monthly OB defines range &amp; direction → refine to weekly → daily → hourly to reduce risk. "You'll know what levels to trade off of" instead of waiting for a neon sign.
