- Fear of loss → fear-based decisions → focus on the negative → trade paralysis. A trader who can't take a loss can't profit long term; losses are a cost of business.
- Framing: bullish OB (high-to-open = most probable support), mean threshold not to be broken on a closing basis, 20-pip stop → 3:1 and 5:1 targets above an old high (+20 pips).
- Account maths ($5,000 account, 10 trades a month):
  - 30% win rate, 3:1, 1% risk: 3×$150 − 7×$50 = +$100 (+2% — great for managed money).
  - 30%, 5:1, 1% risk: 3×$250 − 7×$50 = +$400 (+8%).
  - 30%, 5:1, 2% risk: 3×$500 − 7×$100 = +$800 (+16%; ICT says "$750, 15%").
  - 40%, 5:1, 2%: 4×$500 − 6×$100 = +$1,400 (+28%).
  - 50%, 5:1, 2%: +$2,000 (+40%).
  - 50%, 5:1, 1%: 5×$250 − 5×$50 = +$1,000 (+20%) — "1% makes millionaires"; even 0.5% risk gives ~10%.
- Funds aim for 1–2% a month (≈25–28% a year compounded).
- Position size: dollars per pip = risk $ ÷ stop pips (e.g. $50 ÷ 20 pips = $2.50/pip).
- You never need more than 50% accuracy; accuracy rises naturally with skill.
