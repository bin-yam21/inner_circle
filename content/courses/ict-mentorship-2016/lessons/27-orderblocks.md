- DEFINITION: bullish OB = the LOWEST down-close candle with the most range between open and close, near a support level. VALIDATION: a later candle trades through its HIGH. ENTRY: when price runs away and later returns to the OB high/open (can even enter on the same validating candle's retrace); add ~5 pips for spread. STOP: below the OB low (relatively safe); after the run-away, stop can be raised to just below 50% (mean threshold). Bearish OB = mirror.
- Use candle BODIES (open to close) for the OB; the mean threshold = 50% of the body; the best OBs don't trade below it. Several consecutive down candles = one OB.
- Wait for displacement (smart-money sponsorship); set price ALERTS and wait. Plan risk, size and targets while waiting.
- Buy at INTERNAL range liquidity (the OB inside the range) and sell to EXTERNAL range liquidity (buy stops above the old high).
- Prefer a rally away of 2–3× the OB's body height before expecting a valid retracement. Every new higher down candle becomes the new OB — move your level up.
- LIQUIDITY-BASED BIAS: monthly + weekly + daily bearish → on 4-hour and lower expect retracements UP → sell rallies (raids on buy stops, bearish OBs, FVGs, voids) for low-resistance liquidity runs to lows seen on the daily/weekly. Bullish mirror. The daily is the most dynamic; the monthly changes slowly.
- AVOIDING: when HTF order flow is bullish, DON'T sell bearish OBs — use them only as profit-taking spots (especially near session profit-taking times); otherwise expect price to drive through old highs to external liquidity.
- DOLLAR INDEX example (monthly → weekly → daily, 2016): monthly equal highs above (buy stops); a monthly bullish OB at 94.58 (validated; August 2016 low 94.07, ~50 pips through); another at 95.98; weekly refinements (95.85; lows 95.86/95.87 — 1–2 pips away); ~290–310 pip runs to weekly highs; partial profits and re-adds.
