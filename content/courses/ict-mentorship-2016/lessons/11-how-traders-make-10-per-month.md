- Same AUD/USD case study: daily bullish OB 0.7512, hourly buy stops above → up to ~100 pips of potential (0.7612).
- With the 5-minute framing (stop &lt; 10 pips): risk 2% total; take HALF off at 3R → +3% banked, before the 5-minute buy stops are even taken. The remaining half reached 9R at the 15-minute liquidity pool and ~15R at the hourly pool.
- Arithmetic on a $1,000 account, 10-pip stop: first half at 3:1 (30 pips) = +3%. Runner capturing only HALF of the 100-pip range (50 pips) adds ~21% for the month; the full 100 pips adds ~46%. Even with a 20-pip stop, a 30-pip partial plus a 50-pip runner gives &gt;10%.
- Key ideas: "It's not having big risk that makes the money, it's having small risk." Frame trades at levels with institutional sponsorship (daily/weekly/monthly) because that's where real orders are.
- You don't need the absolute high/low — the "lion's portion" is enough; wanting every pip = greed.
- PAY YOURSELF: always take partials (ICT has watched big open profits turn into losses). The second half always makes more; one-shot-one-kill, about one good weekly trade aiming for ~100 pips.
- 10% a month compounded roughly triples an account in a year (ICT says "over 300%").
