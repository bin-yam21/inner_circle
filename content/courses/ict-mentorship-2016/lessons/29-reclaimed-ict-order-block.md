- Market Maker Buy Model: price swings lower into a higher-timeframe support (old high/low, OB, filled void, FVG), then higher. Left side = sell side of the curve, right side = buy side of the curve.
- Market makers scale into large positions on the way down (hedging). Each small bounce during the decline = minor displacement = evidence of early accumulation. Traders who buy those bounces get stopped out.
- Bullish reclaimed block: a down candle that was used to buy during the sell side of the curve (confirmed by a short-term bounce). On the buy side of the curve, price returns to these same down candles and they are "reclaimed" for new longs. Each buy-side entry matches a sell-side down candle.
- Example: Cable (GBP/USD), decline 24 → 29 November 2016, then reclaimed bullish OBs hit "right to the pip".
- Market Maker Sell Model mirrors it: up candles followed by small declines on the rally (buy side of the curve) get reclaimed as shorts after the high forms (sell side of the curve).
- Homework: on your charts, mark every up candle with a small displacement lower during a rally, and watch the decline revisit them.
