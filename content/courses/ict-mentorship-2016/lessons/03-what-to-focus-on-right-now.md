- Two perspectives (the "market efficiency paradigm"): uninformed/speculative money believes indicators move price and denies there's a smart money; smart money (liquidity providers) knows the uninformed crowd exists and treats everyone else's orders (buy stops, sell stops, pending orders above/below recent highs and lows) as liquidity. "90% of traders lose." Price is delivered to engineer efficiency for smart money.
- Central banks own and price their currency (e.g. the Swiss franc de-peg). Don't vilify market makers or mock retail — think in terms of efficiency.
- Everything in the course frames four price-delivery conditions: expansion, retracement, reversal, consolidation.
- Unlearn previous methods: profit from anything other than institutional order flow is coincidence (ICT made money for nine months by pure luck, then it stopped working).
- ASSIGNMENT — a daily price-action log with charts:
  - Daily chart showing 9–12 months; 4-hour showing 3 months; 1-hour showing 3 weeks; 15-minute showing 3–4 days.
  - Do NOT try to forecast yet.
  - Mark: (1) levels where price moved away QUICKLY; (2) recent highs/lows not yet retested; (3) "clean" highs/lows — equal highs/lows close together (buy stops above / sell stops below are bull's-eyes); (4) which DAY and which killzone (London/New York) the weekly high and low formed; (5) each day's high and low and WHEN they formed.
  - Use one pair that ICT isn't using (he uses EUR and GBP) — his example is USD/CHF. Transpose levels daily → 4-hour → 1-hour on one chart; keep a SEPARATE 15-minute chart with previous days' highs/lows drawn to 0:00 GMT and the day labels. Hourly = the day trader's bellwether.
- Example week (USD/CHF 15-minute): Wednesday ran Tuesday's high (~0.9790) then fell to Tuesday's low; Thursday retraced into Wednesday's range, then dipped below; Friday ran through Thursday's high and the weekly high from Wednesday.
- Keep analysis charts separate from the execution chart so you don't marry your ideas; we trade probabilities, not certainties. ICT still keeps logs after 23 years.
