- NZD futures (19-year blue vs 15-year red): when both lines agree on a sizable swing = high-probability tendency; choppy disagreement = low. NZD/USD moves in the SAME direction as the futures (no inversion, unlike USD/CAD). The June and December contracts show the strongest swings.
- NZD seasonal roadmap: SELL tendencies mid-Feb → mid-March, May → June/July, mid-Aug → Sept/Oct, and early January → February. BUY tendencies (quarterly lows) March–April, June–July, Sept–Oct, plus late Nov → early Dec.
- Two ways to use bearish seasonals: trade them as shorts, OR treat them as the dip that sets up the next BULLISH quarterly shift.
- Never force a seasonal: require technical confirmation (bearish market? rally into a bearish OB? old high run? structure break? overbought premium after 2–3 months up?).
- Year-by-year NZD/USD (weekly):
  - 2007: Feb–Mar and May dips led to March–April and June rallies (to the 0.81 big figure) and a Sept–Oct dip/rally.
  - 2008: highs in Feb–Mar and May, then the financial crisis overrode everything (macro events trump seasonals).
  - 2009: a ~600-pip Feb–Mar decline → strong March–April buy; continuation into the autumn.
  - 2010: a ~700-pip May decline → June–July rally; an August dip into a bullish OB at 0.7000 → ~950 pips to 0.7950; Sept–Oct leg from 0.7400 (~550 pips).
  - 2011: Feb–Mar decline → March–April rally; a ~350-pip May dip to old January highs (resistance turned support); June–July low at a bullish OB near 0.8000 → 0.8800 (~800 pips); Sept–Oct swing 0.7500 → 0.8200 (~700 pips).
  - 2012: ~400-pip Feb–Mar decline, ~800-pip May decline → June low 0.7500 → 0.8450.
  - 2013: Feb–Mar and May declines → June–July low and rally.
  - 2014: no Feb–Mar decline (consolidation broke up); a May retracement → June rally to the 0.88 big figure; mid-Aug → Oct sell-off.
  - 2015: Feb–Mar dip, May decline, weak June–July → Sept–Oct bullish shift from 0.6250 to 0.6850–0.69.
  - 2016: no Feb–Mar decline; a May dip to ~0.6700 (mid-consolidation, bullish OB) → rally to ~0.7450. NZD and AUD held up best against the strong dollar in 2016; the January short-term sell-off also showed.
- Seasonals add the TIME element that indicators lack. Best trades come when seasonal + bullish market + time of day + supportive interest rates + Dollar Index all line up.
