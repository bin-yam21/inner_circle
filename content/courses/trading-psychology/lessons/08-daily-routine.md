A routine removes decisions from the moment of pressure.

## ICT's rhythm
- **Weekend:** big-picture work (macro, rates, intermarket, seasonals) and intermediate work (monthly/weekly/daily levels, COT, sentiment).
- **Each day:** "always start your trading day with reviewing a macro perspective". Check the news calendar, then the short-term picture: DXY SMT, time of day and IPDA.
- **During the killzone:** execute only what you planned.
- **After:** log the trade and the day.

## A sample routine

| Time (NY) | Action |
|---|---|
| Before London | Check-in (sleep, mood, focus). Review rules. Read the news desk. |
| 00:00–05:00 | London window: watch for the Judas swing at your levels. |
| 07:00–10:00 | New York window. Avoid trading in the minute of an 8:30 release. |
| After the session | Log trades, update the daily log, close the platform. |

## Exercises
1. Do the morning **Check-in** before opening any chart, every trading day for two weeks.
2. Run every trade through **Tools → Checklist**, and copy the score into the trade.
