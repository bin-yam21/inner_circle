Fear makes you hesitate, skip valid setups, move stops, and grab profits too early.

## What ICT teaches
- Concern about taking a loss "promotes fear-based decision making". That keeps your focus on the negative and leads to **trade paralysis**.
- Losses are a **cost of doing business**. "No one goes through their career without taking losses."
- The maths frees you. With 5:1 setups and 1% risk, a **30% win rate** still makes +8% over 10 trades. At 50%, it makes +20%. "You never need more than 50% accuracy."

## Practical tools
- **Define the loss before entry.** Fear feeds on the unknown. Know the exact dollar risk, and accept it before you click.
- **Size down until it's boring.** If a loss would hurt, your size is too big. ICT: "1% makes millionaires", and 0.5% is fine while you learn.
- **Pre-commit with orders.** Place the stop and target with the entry, then step away from the chart.

## Exercises
1. Use **Tools → Position size** before every demo trade, and write the dollar risk in the trade note.
2. Do a daily **Check-in** and rate your fear. Compare it with your rule-breaks in the journal.
3. Read your 5 worst trades. Was each loss within plan (acceptable), or caused by breaking a rule (fixable)?
