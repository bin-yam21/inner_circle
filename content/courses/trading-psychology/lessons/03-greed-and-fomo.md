FOMO (fear of missing out) pushes you into late entries in premium, oversized positions and trades with no setup.

## What ICT teaches
- "Greed puts you into the trade… once you're in, greed transfers into fear."
- You don't need the absolute high or low. Take **the lion's portion** of the move: "If you insist on every pip you're going to be frustrated."
- **Don't chase price.** After an expansion, wait for the retracement into the order block or FVG. "You're not going to catch every move."
- **Pay yourself.** Take partials at liquidity pools. "Nobody goes broke taking profits."

## Practical tools
- A **missed trade is not a loss.** Log it in the Daily log with a screenshot. Missed trades show your setup works, so they build patience.
- **One setup per week** is enough (ICT's Reinforcing Liquidity lesson). Set a weekly target, such as 50–75 pips, and stop once you reach it.
- **The premium/discount filter.** If you want to buy but price is above 50% of the range, you are chasing.

## Exercises
1. Tag trades taken out of FOMO with the mistake tag **"Chased price"** and track how they perform.
2. Before entering, say out loud: "Where is the draw on liquidity, and am I in discount or premium?"
