Confidence comes from evidence in your own journal. Overconfidence comes from a few good trades.

## What ICT teaches
- ICT keeps his weekly goal at **50–75 pips**: "every time I try to do more I get a King Kong feeling", and King Kong fell.
- "Coincidence can happen for a long time". He once made money for nine months by pure luck.
- Prove the process in a **demo account** first. A positive result over 30+ trades comes before live money.
- Managed-money standards: 18–25% a year with low drawdown is excellent. You don't need 50% a month.

## Practical tools
- **Size by rules, never by feel.** Risk stays at 1% (of your 30% allocation) after a winning streak too.
- **A streak review.** After 5 wins in a row, re-read your rules and check that you haven't loosened them.
- **Expectancy, not wins.** The Journal shows expectancy (average R per trade); trust that number over your memory.

## Exercises
1. After your next winning streak, compare the checklist scores of those trades with your average.
2. Write down what would make you go live (for example 30 trades, expectancy above 0.3R, max drawdown under 6R) and keep it in **My rules**.
