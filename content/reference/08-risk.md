<ul>
<li><b>After a loss, halve the risk</b> (2% → 1% → 0.5%). At half size, 2R recovers the loss. New traders should exit at break-even-to-equity, especially late in the week. (M2 Mitigate Losing Trades)</li>
<li><b>Allocate only 30% of equity</b> to trading and calculate risk on that slice. For example, $10,000 → $3,000 → 1% = $30. Keep yearly drawdown ≤15%. A realistic goal is 18–25% a year. (M5 Money Management)</li>
<li><b>Stop size is not a skill badge.</b> ICT's intraday maximum is ~30–35 pips, about a third of the average daily range. Position trades may need ~200 pips.</li>
<li><b>Position trades</b> get a trailing stop beyond the 40-day high or low. Tighten to the 20-day level after half to three-quarters of the expected move. Don't rush to break-even. (M5 Position Trade Management)</li>
<li><b>Pay yourself.</b> Take partials at each liquidity pool. "Nobody goes broke taking profits." ICT's own weekly goal is 50–75 pips.</li>
</ul>
