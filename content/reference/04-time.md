<p>From M1 "How Market Makers Condition the Market", "Impulse Price Swings &amp; Protraction" and M3 "Institutional Sponsorship". Times are New York local; this is a bullish-day example.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Power of 3.</b> On a bullish day, buy near or below the <b>New York midnight opening price</b> and sell near the close. In the sponsorship lesson, each day's dip below the midnight open into an OB was bought, and each day closed higher.</li>
<li><b>Protraction rule.</b> If the early-session move goes up, think down, and vice versa.</li>
<li><b>Weekly template.</b> Sunday open is consolidation. Monday expands. A reversal comes Monday or Tuesday, followed by another expansion. Midweek consolidates, then price either reverses or retraces.</li>
<li><b>Daily log assignment (M1).</b> Each day, record the day's high and low and when they formed. For the week, record which day and which session made the high and the low.</li>
</ul>
