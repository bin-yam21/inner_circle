<div class="case">
<p class="when">M2 · AUD/USD · one setup, three lessons</p>
<h3>The daily 0.7512 order block: framing, risk and the 10% maths</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>Higher timeframe.</b> A daily bullish OB at 0.7512, "where banks bought before". On the hourly chart, price ran below an old low into 0.7512 (a turtle soup long). Buy stops above equal highs ~100 pips higher were the target.</li>
<li><b>Hourly frame.</b> Buy 0.7542, stop under the OB mean threshold at 0.7522 (20 pips). One trade a week like this, at 1.5% risk and 1:1, compounds to ~6% a month and doubles the account in a year.</li>
<li><b>15-minute refinement.</b> Buy 0.7520, stop 0.7507 (17 pips).</li>
<li><b>5-minute refinement.</b> Buy ~0.7515 (0.7512 plus spread), stop 0.7507 (8 pips). The trade reached 3R (+24 pips) before even taking the 5-minute buy stops.</li>
<li><b>Scaling out</b> (2% total risk):
  <ul>
    <li>Half off at 3R = +3%.</li>
    <li>The runner reached 9R at the 15-minute pool and ~15R at the hourly pool.</li>
    <li>On a $1,000 account, a runner that catches half the 100-pip range adds ~21%; the full range adds ~46%.</li>
  </ul>
</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="case">
<p class="when">M4 · EUR/USD · 15–19 December 2016</p>
<h3>Void, FVG and a 2-pip gap</h3>
<ul>
<li>A daily FVG sat at ~1.0500–1.0475. A liquidity void ran from 1.0476 down to ~1.0450.</li>
<li>Sell stops under 1.0410 were run first. Then a 75-pip rally filled the void at 1.0476.</li>
<li>The second test made a high of 1.0478 (old high 1.0477). Bodies then gapped from a close of 1.0472 to an open of 1.0470.</li>
<li>A sell limit at 1.0470 had almost no drawdown. It covered at the sell stops near 1.0404, and then at 1.0365.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h3>Buying the sell stops</h3>
<ul>

<li>A buy limit at 1.3090 filled; the low printed 1.3083, only 7 pips of heat.</li>
<li>Price ran to the swing-high buy stops at 1.3360, about 270 pips.</li>

<div class="case">
<p class="when">M5 · USD/JPY · 2016 position trades</p>
<h3>Weekly arrays, daily entries, 40/20-day trailing stops</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>Short.</b> Weekly bearish OB (open 121.69, high 121.72), stop above the 40-day high (~260 pips). The trade reached about 8R.</li>
<li><b>Long.</b> In Nov 2016, price traded below the monthly candle bodies (a monthly rejection block, equilibrium ~99.00) into two weekly bullish OBs. Buying below each down-close daily candle produced moves of 1,800, 980, 785, 600, 500 and 360 pips into the weekly bearish OB: open 118.61, actual high 118.66.</li>
<li><b>Stop management.</b> The stop trailed under the 40-day low (109.19 held through the election whipsaw), then under the 20-day low once price passed the range midpoint.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="case">
<p class="when">M3 · EUR/USD · monthly, 2008–2010</p>
<h3>A 13-month set-up, a 6-month payoff</h3>
<ul>
<li>EUR/USD collapsed from ~1.5950 to ~1.2450.</li>
<li>Over a year later, price returned to the last up candle before that drop: the monthly bearish OB near 1.5080.</li>
<li>The target was the "too clean" equal lows and bodies near 1.2200, where trend-following funds keep their stops.</li>
<li>The move was ~2,900 pips. On the weekly and daily, ICT sold breakers, OBs, OTE retracements and turtle soups along the way.</li>
</ul>
</div>
