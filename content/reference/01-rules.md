<p>These are the ideas he repeats across many lessons. Each cites where it comes from.</p>
<ol class="rules">
<li>Markets move from one pool of stops to the other: buy stops sit above old highs, sell stops below old lows. Smart money sells to buy stops and buys from sell stops.<span class="src">M1 Liquidity Runs · M4 Liquidity Pools</span></li>
<li>Buy only below 50% of the current range (discount), ideally in the 62–79% OTE. Sell only above 50% (premium). Never buy at 50% just because a Fib says so.<span class="src">M1 Equilibrium vs Discount / Premium</span></li>
<li>There are only four market conditions. Consolidation is always followed by expansion. After expansion comes either a retracement or a reversal.<span class="src">M1 Elements · How Market Makers Condition</span></li>
<li>Use candle bodies, not wicks. Bodies carry the institutional volume, and stop runs only need to clear the bodies.<span class="src">M3 Institutional Order Flow · M4 Rejection Block</span></li>
<li>A bullish order block is the last down candle before a displacement. It is confirmed when price trades through its high. The best ones never close beyond the mean threshold (50% of the body).<span class="src">M4 Orderblocks · Propulsion</span></li>
<li>Enter at internal range liquidity (an OB or FVG inside the range). Exit at external range liquidity (stops beyond the range).<span class="src">M4 Reinforcing Liquidity</span></li>
<li>The market turns in only two ways: a stop run (breaker swing) or a failure swing. Sell at an old high, or on the retracement to the structure break. Buy at an old low, or on the return to the break.<span class="src">M5 Institutional Swing Points</span></li>
<li>The algorithm references the last 20, 40 and 60 trading days. Their highs and lows are the targets, and a new shift is expected within 60 days.<span class="src">M5 Quarterly Shifts · Open Float</span></li>
<li>Every 3–4 months the market shifts (reverses, retraces deeply, or consolidates). Every quarter offers an intermediate-term trade.<span class="src">M3 Macro→Micro · M5 Quarterly Shifts</span></li>
<li>Interest rates drive currencies: "money seeks yield". Falling bond prices mean rising yields, which supports the dollar. Check the 30/10/5-year triad at key Dollar Index levels.<span class="src">M4 Interest Rate Effects · M5 10-Year Notes</span></li>
<li>A high-reward setup needs 7 things in agreement: 2 of 4 big-picture factors, 2 of 3 intermediate factors, and 1 from each of 3 short-term factors.<span class="src">M2 Secrets To Selecting Setups</span></li>
<li>Risk ≤ 1–2%. Halve your risk after a loss. Take partials ("pay yourself"). Small risk, not big risk, makes the money.<span class="src">M2 lessons · M5 Money Management</span></li>
</ol>
<p class="quote">"Price has no awareness of your trend line… Price only respects where the actual liquidity is in the marketplace." (M3, Trend Lines)</p>
