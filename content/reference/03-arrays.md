<p>From M5 "Defining HTF PD Arrays". Mark these on the monthly, weekly and daily charts. Starting at equilibrium, scan the left side of the chart for each one in order. If one isn't there, check it off and look for the next.</p>
<div class="tw"><table>
<thead><tr><th>Order price meets it</th><th>Premium arrays (going up)</th><th>Discount arrays (going down)</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td class="n">1st</td><td>Bearish mitigation block</td><td>Bullish mitigation block</td></tr>
<tr><td class="n">2nd</td><td>Bearish breaker (takes precedence)</td><td>Bullish breaker (takes precedence)</td></tr>
<tr><td class="n">3rd</td><td>Liquidity void</td><td>Liquidity void</td></tr>
<tr><td class="n">4th</td><td>Fair value gap</td><td>Fair value gap</td></tr>
<tr><td class="n">5th</td><td>Bearish order block (almost always present)</td><td>Bullish order block</td></tr>
<tr><td class="n">6th</td><td>Rejection block (just above the candle bodies)</td><td>Rejection block (just below the bodies)</td></tr>
<tr><td class="n">7th</td><td>Old high or old long-term low (deepest premium)</td><td>Old low or old long-term high (deepest discount)</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
<p><b>Breaker rule.</b> If a breaker sits between price and a deeper array, expect price to stop at the breaker. The void, gap or OB beyond it will often stay untouched.</p>
<p><b>Failed level rule.</b> If a daily level fails, look out to the weekly. If the weekly fails, look to the monthly. On US election day 2016, daily levels in USD/JPY broke, but price went straight into the lower weekly bullish OB. (M5 Trade Conditions)</p>
<div class="tw"><table>
<thead><tr><th>Other blocks</th><th>Definition in the lessons</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td><b>Rejection block</b></td><td>A swing high or low with long wicks. The zone runs from the highest (or lowest) body to the wick extreme. Sell when price returns to the body level, or sell on a stop below the body after a poke above it.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Propulsion block</b></td><td>A down candle that traded into an earlier bullish OB. It is very sensitive: price should only touch its high, and its mean threshold should never break, so it allows an ultra-tight stop.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Vacuum block</b></td><td>A gap from news (NFP, FOMC) or a session open. Price either fills it 100% ("perfect delivery") or stops at an OB inside it. An 8:30 a.m. gap usually fills the same day; a gap after 10–11 a.m. often waits.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Reclaimed OB</b></td><td>Market maker buy model: down candles bought during the fall (each small bounce marks one) get bought again as the market rises.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Mitigation block</b></td><td>The last up candle smart money sold to push price below lows. When price returns to it, they cover shorts and add longs, which produces an explosive move.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>Liquidity void</b></td><td>Long one-sided candles. A void can stay open for a session or for months. A drop with no buying means "a void of buy-side liquidity".</td></tr>
</tbody></table></div>
